What this is

LOLBAS catalogs abusable binaries. LOTS catalogs abusable domains. LOLPHISH catalogs the layer in between and beyond: the legitimate mechanisms — auth flows, notification pipelines, hosting surfaces, social-engineering primitives — that phishing industrialized on top of.

Entry anatomy

Each entry documents the legitimate purpose, the abuse primitive, named variants, known kits and actors, trust surfaces, ATT&CK mappings, detection telemetry, and the structural mitigation — not just the IOC of the week.

Defensive use only

Everything here is publicly documented research, structured for detection engineering and threat modeling. If you find a technique useful, your job is to go kill it in your environment.
